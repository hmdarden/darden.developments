About me

What I Work With

My toolkit includes Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, Google Analytics, Figma, Adobe Creative Cloud, WordPress, HTML/CSS, and collaborative platforms like Confluence, Asana, and Monday.com. I use these tools to develop creative content, plan and manage digital campaigns, refine audience targeting, and analyze performance. Beyond the tools themselves, my strengths lie in blending creativity with strategy—developing engaging content, connecting messages with the right audiences, and translating data into actionable insights. Whether I'm shaping a campaign, recommending creative approaches, or evaluating results, I focus on making communication both meaningful and effective.

My Foundation

I earned my MA in Professional and Technical Writing from the University of Arkansas at Little Rock, with an emphasis on Digital Content Strategy, Audience Research, and User Experience. I also hold a BA in Web Design & Development, along with certificates in Digital Marketing, Google Analytics, and Google Project Management. My background in content creation, creative design, digital strategy, UX, and analytics allows me to combine creative thinking with audience insights to develop engaging content and strategic campaigns that deliver measurable results.

Beyond the Work

When I’m not working, you’ll find me reading sci-fi/fantasy or in a studio metalsmithing, doing stained glass, or sculpting. For me, physical design is another way to practice problem-solving and making things clearer, more beautiful, and more useful.
