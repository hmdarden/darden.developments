Community Event Response Campaign

Project Description / Goals:

This anonymized paid social campaign focused on generating interest and event responses for a community event through strategic audience targeting and campaign management.

  1. Generate cost-efficient event responses through targeted paid social advertising.
  2. Connect with relevant audiences and encourage interest in the event.
  3. Support community sharing and engagement with promotional content.
  4. Monitor and optimize performance throughout the campaign.

Campaign Strategy & Management:

I managed the paid social campaign from setup through performance reporting, focusing on efficient event response generation and relevant audience targeting. My responsibilities included campaign execution, budget pacing, performance monitoring, and optimization.

  1. Developed an audience targeting approach aligned with the event's promotional objectives.
  2. Managed campaign execution through Meta Ads Manager.
  3. Monitored event response costs, engagement, and budget pacing throughout the campaign.
  4. Reviewed campaign performance to identify opportunities for optimization.
  5. Evaluated sharing activity as an indicator of audience interest and content redistribution.

Skills & Tools

  • Meta Ads Manager
  • Paid Social Strategy
  • Audience Targeting
  • Campaign Management
  • Budget Pacing
  • Performance Analysis
  • Campaign Reporting

Campaign Results:

  • 8,090 event responses
  • $0.12 average cost per event response
  • 1,700 post shares
  • 2,420 reactions
  • 156 comments
  • 8,234 page engagements

The campaign demonstrated cost-efficient event response generation and substantial community interaction. Sharing created opportunities for event information to circulate beyond the original paid targeting.

Event responses do not represent confirmed attendance, and additional unique reach from sharing was not independently verified.
