Social Media Campaign:
UA - Little Rock Grad School

Project Description / Goals:

  1. Maintain and expand an ongoing themed social media campaign targeting graduate students
  2. Create consistent, relatable content to motivate, support, and inform the graduate student community

Creative Process and Strategy:

  1. Wrote all post copy for recurring themes:
    • #MotivationMondays
    • #WellnessWednesdays
    • #GradFactFridays
  2. Designed simple, cohesive static images to complement each post
  3. Planned a semester-long content calendar to ensure variety and relevance
  4. Maintained a steady posting schedule , publishing most Mondays, Wednesdays, and Fridays
  5. Focused on consistency to boost audience engagement and strengthen the Graduate School’s online presence

  • Provided ongoing motivational, wellness, and informational content for graduate students, contributing to a supportive online environment
