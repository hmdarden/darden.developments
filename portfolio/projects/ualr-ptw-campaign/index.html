Social Media Campaign:
Professional & Technical Writing

Project Description / Goals:

  1. Highlight the range of opportunities available to students interested in professional and technical writing.
  2. Create approachable, relevant content about writing, career paths, and the program.
  3. Build a consistent social presence using recognizable university branding and audience-focused messaging.

Creative Process and Strategy:

  1. Developed social media post concepts and copy around writing, career possibilities, and student interests.
  2. Created branded static graphics that aligned with the university's visual identity.
  3. Planned varied content themes , including career spotlights, questions for writers, and timely posts.
  4. Adapted writing for social media to keep information concise, engaging, and accessible.
  5. Prepared posts for a consistent content schedule with attention to deadlines and visual cohesion.
