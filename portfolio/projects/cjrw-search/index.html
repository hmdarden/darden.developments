Audience-Driven Community Engagement

Project Description / Goals:

This paid social campaign demonstrated how audience targeting and relevant promotional messaging can support community engagement. The campaign generated 625 post shares and 1,157 reactions.

  1. Connect with relevant audiences through targeted paid social advertising.
  2. Encourage meaningful interaction with community-focused content.
  3. Support audience sharing and opportunities for broader visibility.
  4. Evaluate campaign engagement and audience response.

Campaign Strategy & Management:

I managed paid social execution, audience targeting, and performance monitoring. Engagement data helped me evaluate how audiences responded to promotional content and informed campaign reporting and strategic recommendations.

  1. Developed audience targeting recommendations aligned with the campaign's objectives.
  2. Managed campaign execution through Meta Ads Manager.
  3. Monitored engagement metrics, including reactions, shares, saves, and page engagements.
  4. Reviewed audience interactions to identify patterns in campaign performance.
  5. Applied campaign findings to reporting and strategic recommendations.

Skills & Tools

  • Meta Ads Manager
  • Paid Social Strategy
  • Audience Targeting
  • Campaign Optimization
  • Engagement Analysis
  • Performance Reporting

Campaign Results:

  • 625 post shares
  • 1,157 reactions
  • 19 saves
  • Approximately 3,922 page engagements

The campaign demonstrated audience interaction through reactions, saves, and sharing. Post shares also created opportunities for community-focused content to circulate through personal networks.

Additional unique reach resulting from sharing was not independently verified.
