Community Sharing & Event Visibility

Project Description / Goals:

This paid social campaign highlighted how audience sharing can support community event visibility. The campaign generated substantial engagement, including 776 post shares.

  1. Increase event awareness among relevant community audiences.
  2. Connect promotional messaging with audiences likely to be interested.
  3. Encourage interaction and sharing of event-related content.
  4. Evaluate audience engagement to inform campaign reporting.

Campaign Strategy & Management:

I managed paid social campaign execution with a focus on reaching relevant audiences and evaluating how they interacted with the promotional content. I also provided messaging and creative recommendations and incorporated engagement findings into reporting.

  1. Developed audience and messaging recommendations aligned with the campaign's objectives.
  2. Managed campaign delivery through Meta Ads Manager.
  3. Monitored reactions, comments, saves, and post shares to evaluate audience interaction.
  4. Reviewed engagement performance to understand how audiences responded to promotional messaging.
  5. Incorporated campaign findings into reporting and future recommendations.

Skills & Tools

  • Meta Ads Manager
  • Paid Social Strategy
  • Audience Targeting
  • Messaging Recommendations
  • Engagement Analysis
  • Campaign Reporting

Campaign Results:

  • 776 post shares
  • 835 reactions
  • 56 saves
  • 39 comments

Sharing was a notable campaign outcome, creating opportunities for event information to circulate through personal networks beyond directly targeted audiences.

Post shares indicate redistribution but do not independently establish how many additional unique people saw the content.
